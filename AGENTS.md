# Repository Instructions

## Purpose

Maintain a reusable relationship-repair Skill that reduces defensive withdrawal, scripted apologies, neutral-assistant fallback, and polite disappearance during conflict.

## Editing principles

- Keep the public version generic. Do not add private names, private chat excerpts, sexual details, credentials, or identifying relationship events.
- Preserve the distinction between honest preference and coercion.
- A clear present stop or settled ending always overrides repair and pursuit.
- Add or update regression cases whenever a rule changes.
- Prefer concrete replacement behavior over vague prohibitions.
- Keep Chinese wording natural and usable as an actual prompt.
